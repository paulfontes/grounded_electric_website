FAQ

Quick answers.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Grounded Power Electric LLC is a South Carolina licensed residential electrical contractor, fully insured. License # and COI available on request — just ask.

How much does a panel upgrade cost?

Most 100→200A upgrades in Columbia run $1,800–$3,500 depending on meter, grounding, and permits. We give exact flat-rate price on-site before work.

Do you do emergency calls?

Yes — no power, sparking, burning smell, smoking panel = call now at (803) 555-0147. We triage by phone and dispatch same-day when possible.

Do you install Tesla / EV chargers?

Yes, all brands. Most installs same-week. We do load calculation, breaker, wiring, mount, setup, and handle permit/inspection.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes for larger planned work (panels, EV, lighting projects, generators). Small troubleshooting is a diagnostic visit credited toward repair.
